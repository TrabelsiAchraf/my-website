App Stats

Download numbers for my personal iOS apps, straight from Apple's Sales & Trends reports — exact figures, refreshed daily. Every number has an i next to it: tap it for a plain-English explanation, or read the full glossary at the bottom.

What these numbers mean

First-time downloads
People who installed the app for the very first time on their Apple ID. Reinstalls and updates are counted separately, so this number never double-counts the same user.
Last 7 days / Last 30 days
First-time downloads over the 7 (or 30) most recent days Apple has published. Apple's reports run about 24 h behind, so "today" is never included. The 30-day figure is the one to trust for a trend — at these volumes the 7-day figure swings on a single download.
vs previous 7d
The last 7 days compared with the 7 days before them. ▲ means picking up, ▼ means slowing down, "new" means there was nothing to compare against.
Monthly downloads (All time)
One bar per calendar month, rebuilt from Apple's monthly sales reports — the full history, well beyond the 90-day window. The last bar is the month in progress and is shown faded, since it is still filling up. Apple only keeps monthly reports for 12 months; anything older is recovered from the yearly report, which has no month breakdown, so it counts towards the all-time total and appears as a "+N before that" note next to the chart rather than as bars.
Released / Current version
Read live from Apple's public storefront: the day the first version shipped, and the version on sale today with its release date.
Daily downloads chart
One point per day over the last 90 days. Hover the chart — or drag a finger across it on a phone — to read the exact count for any day.
Updates installed
How many times an existing user installed a newer version of the app. These are not new users: it is a retention signal — people who still have the app on their device and keep it current. An app that has never shipped a second version shows 0.
Re-downloads
Installs by an Apple ID that had already downloaded the app before: a second device, a restored backup, or a reinstall after deleting it. Apple reports them separately from downloads.
Active devices
Devices that opened the app recently, from Apple's App Analytics. It only covers users who agreed to share analytics with developers, and Apple withholds it entirely for low-traffic apps — hence "not published by Apple" on most cards.
Devices
Which device the download came from. "Desktop" means a Mac — either a Mac App Store download, or an iPhone/iPad app installed on Apple Silicon.
Countries
The App Store storefront the download came from. That follows the buyer's Apple ID country, not necessarily where they physically are.

Source: Apple's Sales & Trends reports — exact download counts, not the opt-in Analytics samples — pulled every morning by a GitHub Action, with ~24 h latency. No revenue figures: the apps are free.
